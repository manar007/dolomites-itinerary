High point
⚠️ Your hardest day, and the highest altitude of the trip
  • Expect an early start, probably in the dark. Have your headlamp ready.
  • It is cold at the top. Wear layers and keep the TNF shell handy for the wind.
  • The sun is strong at 4,500 m. Wear the Julbo Fury and sunscreen.
  • Carry enough water and snacks for 9–10 hours of walking.
  • The 1,600 m descent is hard on the knees. Go slowly, and use trekking poles if you have them.
  • Tell the guide straight away about a headache, nausea or dizziness.
